## 2026-11-16: Section drawings mark the cut, so a part's two sides read as one piece (no result changed)

The README's and `docs/reference.md`'s side-cut drawings (`scripts/failure_figures.py`: the three wheels, the M3 snap-lip knob and the three tripod plates) are cut through the center. The cut goes through the bore or hole, so a round part's two sides looked like two separate, detached pieces. Dan pointed at the wheel under "Pass: a cone roof".

Each side cut is now titled "Section: cut through the center" and its cut faces are hatched. Behind the cut, the half of the part beyond the cutting plane is drawn in light gray, as a drafter draws a section, so the bore or hole wall that joins the two cut faces fills the gap between them. Where the cut comes out in more than one piece there is also a dash-dot center line through the middle of the gap and the note "one piece: the light gray behind the cut joins both sides around the axis" (wheels and the knob, which also get a dashed ellipse for the rim) or "... around the hole" (the tripod plates). The wheel legend names "Wheel, cut face" and "Wheel behind the cut".

Checked with the two decisions models (`pbe/judges/decisions.py`: Jev 1.14 and GPT-6 Luna Decisions, $0.02 in all, outside `results/` because it grades a drawing, not a design), each drawing judged alone with neutral yes/no questions, then old and new shown side by side in random order. Average p(yes) for "would a reader see two detached pieces?": old 0.51 (Jev) and 0.54 (Luna); new 0.10 and 0.05. "Clearly one connected part?": old 0.37 and 0.12; new 0.90 and 1.00. Both picked the new drawing as clearer in 7 of 7 pairs. With the title and note removed (a control for the models just reading the words), "one connected part" stays at 0.77 and 0.74 and "two detached pieces" at 0.15 and 0.07; before the part behind the cut was drawn, the same control gave 0.66 and 0.41 for "one part", which is why it was added.

Only the drawing changed: the same meshes, cut at the same plane. The cross-cut, strand and gallery images and every grade, count and table are unchanged; `scripts/update_tables.sh --check` and the results site tests pass. The results site's own drawn views (`scripts/results_site_views.py`) are not touched by this change.
