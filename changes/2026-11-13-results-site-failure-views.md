## 2026-11-13: The results site draws what each failing check looked at (no result changed)

On many trials the render didn't show the failure: only `bridges`, the knob's nut pocket and `connected_bodies` were marked in red, and Estoa's R3, R5 and R6 had no picture at all (Opus 5.5's Estoa trial 4 was Dan's example). Each failing or not-graded check on the results site now carries a drawing of what the check looked at, built from the grader's own functions and numbers, never a second grading. No verdict, `grades.json` or `summary.csv` changes.

- **`scripts/results_site_views.py`** draws one figure per finding for every saved design and uploads it; `scripts/results_site_views.json` records the links and sizes. It started from the per-check views drawn for the Estoa review and reuses `scripts/failure_figures.py`. The kinds: the layer under a roof with its strands for `bridges`; the nut in its pocket with the recorded prints on a number line for the knob's R3 and R4; a cut down the axle with the bearings and bolt drawn in for the wheel; a cut through the bore for the tripod plate; the port, the board, the screws and the keyholes (per the R6 check as loosened today) for the Estoa box.
- **Coverage, counted failures:** 742 have a drawing and 92 don't. Of the 92, 57 are designs whose code didn't run, so there is no shape; 10 are five wheels that built as a sheet 0.00 mm thick; the rest are meshes the grader's own cut can't read. Each one says why on the page. 229 not-graded checks are drawn the same way and marked not graded.
- **`scripts/results_site.py`** (schema 3) attaches each drawing to its check; `tests/test_results_site.py` checks that every counted failed check has a drawing or a stated reason, and `tests/test_results_site_views.py` covers the view chosen per check, the empty-cut case and that a legend names only what is drawn.
- **The page** shows the drawing with its check, and the first failure's drawing beside the render at the top of a failing trial.
- **Limits.** A fit that misses by 0.05 to 0.2 mm can't be seen at true scale, so the number line carries it. On a phone the text inside a drawing is small until it's enlarged. The tripod's clamp jaw and bolt head aren't drawn because their sizes aren't in the catalog. The audit of these drawings by a decisions model is in its own entry.
