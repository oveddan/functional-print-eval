## 2026-11-14: Results site: five fixes from the second design review (no result changed)

No verdict, count or saved result changes. Each fix is to how the page shows what is saved.

- **A step from the other trial of a pair no longer sits under the wrong verdict.** A link with `phase=second` on a failed first print showed the passing second print under the first print's title and a bare "Fail". When the step shown is saved as another trial, the header now says so, names that trial and shows its verdict, and the page's own verdict is dimmed; picking one of the trial's own steps removes the note (`otherStepNote` in `scripts/results_site/detail.js`). Looked at on Opus 5.5, Estoa light, trial 4 (first print fails, second passes).
- **The small bars are the pass rate.** The bars in the table and in "By model and part" drew every design (pass, not graded, didn't pass) at full width, so every bar was the same length. Their filled length is now the share of the cell's designs that pass, on a track, the same rule as the chart. Not graded is no part of a bar; a table cell says "N not graded" in words and a by-part cell says it in its tooltip and label. The legend lost its two keys for what is no longer drawn.
- **The chart says what a first-print-only row measures.** For the models with no second prints the row's percentage was labelled "after one round of feedback" and its cost "first print plus feedback". Those rows now say first print, the divider above them says what their percentage and cost are, and the column header reads "Pass rate".
- **The trial dialog arrives solid.** It faded in from transparent, which showed the page through a half-drawn trial. It still rises 6 px; the backdrop still fades.
- **Reason thumbnails that are a check's drawing** showed the whole sheet at 96 by 72 px, unreadable and glaring white in the dark theme. The thumbnail now shows the sheet's first panel enlarged, and is inverted in the dark theme (the full drawing on the trial page is unchanged).
- **Checks.** Four new tests in `tests/test_results_site.py`. The page's own count check reads 0 problems of 1,973 comparisons after the change. Looked at in the dark theme at desktop width only.
