## 2026-11-13: The results site's scorecard has a chart of first print and after feedback, with cost per passing design (no result changed)

Dan asked for the scorecard as a graph: a stacked bar per model for the first try and with feedback, and the dollars per passing design beside it. The chart sits above the table, which stays. No verdict, count or saved result changes; the chart draws numbers `scripts/results_site.py` already wrote from `scripts/scorecard.py`'s own rows.

- **The bars.** One bar per model, all of its counted designs for the four scored parts: pass on the first print, pass on the second print after feedback (marked with a plus), not graded (hatched, never a pass), and those that didn't pass (fail, declined or no design). The last row adds up the headline's seven models: 105 on the first print and 46 more after feedback, 151 of 281, the README's numbers.
- **Order.** By pass rate after feedback, highest first, said in a line above the chart. The five models with no second prints sit under a divider, "First print only: no second prints were run", by first-print pass rate. The table keeps its own order.
- **Cost per passing design** is a labelled value beside each bar, not a second axis: what the API provider charged for every first print in the bar plus each second print counted in it (`Row.cost(after=True)`), divided by the designs that pass after feedback. Print time and filament aren't in it. A model with no passing design would show no value rather than zero. From the saved runs: Gemini 4 Argon $0.19, Opus 5.5 $0.36, Gemini 3.8 Flash $0.25, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash $0.09, GPT-6.1 Sol $0.17, Sonnet 5.5 $0.29; first print only: GLM 5.3 Flash $0.17, GPT-6 Luna $0.06 (3 passes), DeepSeek V4 Pro $2.75 (2), Qwen 3.8 Flash $1.77 (1), Haiku 5.5 $0.22 (1); the seven headline models together $0.23 ($34.07 over 151 passes). A value from fewer than 5 passes is shown muted with "from N passes", so $0.06 from 3 passes doesn't read as the best. The chart's header says "first print plus feedback" and the table's cost column now says "with test prints first", because the two are different conditions with different values (GPT-6.1 Sol: $0.17 and $0.08).
- **Why a value and not a line.** The costs run from $0.06 to $2.75, so on one scale nine of the eleven models would sit in the first eighth of it, and a second scale over the bars could be misread as the bars' own.
- **Links.** Each piece of a bar opens exactly the designs it counts, and each model name opens that model's trials. Two verdict filter values were added for this, since no single verdict could say either: `didnt-pass` (fail, declined or no design together) and `second-pass` (after feedback only: passes on the second print). Each shows as a pressed chip in the filters when a link sets it.
- **Checks.** `window.resultsSiteCheck` counts each chart row from the listed trials (designs, both kinds of pass, not graded, those that didn't pass, the cost in cents and per pass) and checks that every piece's link opens a list as long as the piece's number: 0 problems of 1,913 comparisons on today's build. Two tests in `tests/test_results_site.py` cover the counts and the cost from the saved runs.
- **Colour.** In the chart what didn't pass is drawn hollow, not the table's faint red: the dataviz palette check put that red and the second-print green at a difference of 1.1 for a reader who can't tell red from green (8 is the target), and in the chart the two sit side by side. The legends and the line under each bar call it "didn't pass" in both the chart and the table; the table's small bars still use the faint red.
- **Limits.** A first print that passes only since a regrade had a second print paid for that the scorecard no longer counts; its cost is left out, as in `scripts/scorecard.py`. Pieces narrower than 6% of a bar carry no number on them; the line under the bar has every count. Checked by eye at 1280 and 375 px wide in light and dark; not checked with a screen reader or in forced-colours mode.
