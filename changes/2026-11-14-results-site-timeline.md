## 2026-11-14: Results site: a timeline on each trial, with the design as it stood at each step (no result changed)

A trial's page now has a timeline of what happened, in order: the measurements the model asked for, each round of test prints it asked the person for, its first print, what it was told, its second print. Picking a step puts that step's version where the picture and the checks are. No verdict, `grades.json` or count changes; the page only shows saved records in a new order.

- **The phases** come from `scripts/results_site_timeline.py`, which `scripts/results_site.py` calls for each trial's detail (`timeline`). The order of questions and test-print rounds is read from the saved transcript; where the transcript's calls don't add up to the record's (1 of the 708 entries that asked anything), measurements are listed first. A first print and its second print carry the same timeline, so opening the second print lands on the last step of it. 1053 of 1196 entries have more than one phase; the other 143 only handed in a design and get no timeline.
- **What each step shows.** A design step (first print, second print, final design) shows that design's render or 3D view, its parts, its assembled view and its checks, with its saved verdict in the step's label. A test-print round shows a render of each piece the model asked for beside what the simulated person said. A step with no shape (questions asked, what the person said after the first print, a build error sent back) shows the words and says there is no picture for it.
- **Renders of the asked-for test prints** are new: `scripts/results_site_media.py --rounds` builds each `print_test` request again from the code in the saved transcript, renders each piece and uploads it; `scripts/results_site_media_rounds.json` holds the links. 224 trials asked for 294 rounds and 486 pieces: 478 pieces are drawn, and 8 pieces in 7 rounds aren't, because the model's code for them doesn't run (5 of those rounds have no picture at all, 2 show the pieces that did build). The page says so on those rounds. These are renders of what the model asked the person to print; the person's reply comes from Dan's recorded test prints, and nothing was printed.
- **The control** (`scripts/results_site/timeline.js`) is a row of tabs: arrow keys, Home and End move between steps, each step has a visible label and a one-line summary, and Earlier and Later buttons step through by touch. The step is kept in the address as `phase=`, and Copy link carries it. The 3D choice, part tabs, Assembled view, Play the fit, STL download and check cards work as before inside a step.
- **Checks.** `tests/test_results_site.py` has 6 new tests: every timeline accounts for exactly the measurements and rounds its record holds and states each design's saved verdict (so not graded never reads as a pass), a first print and its second print share one timeline, a design-only trial has one phase, the order follows the transcript, and a round's renders are of the pieces that round asked for. The page repeats the count check in the browser for every trial it loads (`window.resultsSiteCheck.detail`), 0 problems on the trials opened.
- **Looked at** on a first print with a second print (GPT-6.1 Sol, Estoa light, trial 4), a test-first trial with two rounds (Gemini 4 Argon, knob M3, trial 2) and a passing design-only trial (Haiku 5.5, knob M5, trial 1), at desktop width in light and dark and at 375 px in light. Still weak: the 375 px dark view was only measured (no sideways scroll, 44 px targets), not seen clean, because the shared preview pane kept being hidden; the page's heading keeps the opened trial's verdict while another step of the pair is shown; a test-print render has no red marks, since no check is saved per asked-for piece; and with five or more steps a phone shows about three at a time and the rest by scrolling the strip.

Codex's review found one fault, fixed before merging. In `renderWithTimeline` the guard `() => turn === shown` didn't change when the trial was closed or replaced, so a phase that finished loading late could draw into a detached panel and call the global `teardown()`, disposing the next trial's 3D view. The guard now also requires the panel to still be on the page (`control.panel.isConnected`). A line in `tests/test_results_site.py` checks it. Merged on top of #32: `--rounds` and `scripts/results_site_media_rounds.json` are outside #32's content keys and `--check`, which is right, because the rounds come from the code in the transcript and a regrade can't make them out of date.
