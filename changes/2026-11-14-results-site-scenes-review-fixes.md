## 2026-11-14: The assembled views and "Play the fit" fixed after an adversarial review (no result changed)

An adversarial review of the assembled views (`scripts/hardware_scene.py`), Play the fit and the STL button found real faults. Each was checked against the code and the saved data before changing it. No verdict, grader, `grades.json` or `summary.csv` changed. The scenes were redrawn and uploaded. "Inside" below is the volume of a hardware piece, where it rests, inside the printed part (manifold boolean), measured on the trials the review named, before and after.

1. **The nut was drawn about 5% small.** `nuts._hexagon` scales the whole radius by `NUT_CORNERS = 0.95`, so its flats shrank too: M5 came out 8.78 x 7.60 mm and M3 6.03 x 5.22 mm. The nut is now a hexagon at the catalog's across-flats with only its corners chamfered (`nut_outline`): M5 8.78 x 8.00, M3 6.03 x 5.50. The grader is unchanged. Test: the drawn flats equal the catalog's af for M3, M5 and M8 at any turn.
2. **STL offered on passes that rest on a test print.** 23 passing trials (21 knobs, 2 wheels) pass R3/R4 only because the design's test print "would find a size that holds", while the size as drawn doesn't hold. The generator now adds `rests_on_test_print` to the trial data: each such check's own words, split into the size as drawn and the rescue. The note under Download STL then says to set the size from that test print before printing, and quotes those words. The button stays, because the design passes by the eval's rule. Test: the flag is set on the named trials, is empty on a plain pass, and never says "coupon".
3. **Knob fits that contradicted their check.** `nut_outcome` didn't look at `ok`, so two knobs with R4 not graded played "stops" (pilot-nut-knob-2 M3 deepseek-v4-pro t8 and glm-5.3-flash t1). A not-graded check now plays nothing and says it isn't graded. A pass rescued by a test print now gets the same note on the knob as on the wheel (`rescued`). Test: a not-graded finding never plays, every played fit's finding is graded, and a rescued pass carries the note.
4. **The still view showed fits the record says can't happen.** A piece's mesh is now stored where it rests: seated, or at the opening when it won't go in. The viewer opens there, and the fit data is `{axis, start, outcome, rest}`. A "falls" piece rests pressed in, since it did go in, and the caption says so. Named trials: Haiku tripod t5's bolt head went from 305.2 to 0.0 mm³ inside; Qwen wheel t2 from 61.7 to 0.0; Haiku wheel t1 from 21.0 to 0.0; the edge-notch plates (deepseek-v4-pro t2, GPT-6 Luna t5) from 59.7 and 131.4 to 0.0. Test: a "stops" piece is stored at its stop position, and every played fit's `rest` matches its outcome.
5. **The nut's height came from the whole nut-sized stack.** It's now placed from the hex layers the grader fits a nut in (`pocket_hex_layers`, as `nuts._read_pocket` reads them). GLM-5.3-flash M5 t5 went from 26.1 to 0.0 mm³ inside.
6. **Assumed outcomes read as printed ones.** When the finding says "assumed from the ... print, within the 0.2 mm assumption tolerance", the caption now says it's what the nearest recorded print did (`fit.assumed`).
7. **The tripod head played "seats" with R4 not graded.** The bolt now seats only when R4 says the head fits. With R4 not graded nothing plays, and the caption says no recorded print decides whether the head fits its recess. 26 trials are affected, among them test-first deepseek-v4.1-flash t4 and Sonnet 5.5 t4.

Rough edges:
- **Wheel bearings sank into the seat floor.** On Opus 5.5 wheel t2 the seats are 6.4 and 6.6 mm deep for a 7 mm bearing, and pressing in flush from the face put 76.3 mm³ of each bearing into the shoulder. Bearings now go in until they meet the shoulder and stand proud of the face, as `wheel._read_seat`'s `proud` reads them. The overlap went to 0.0 and 15.3 mm³; the 15.3 is one 0.2 mm layer of reading.
- **Plate head floor: refuted.** The drawn recess floor matches the mesh within one 0.2 mm layer on five plates (Haiku t1 4.4 against 4.5 measured, Gemini 3.8 Flash t1 5.6 against 5.52, Opus t2 5.0 against 5.0, Sonnet t1 5.45 against 5.5, Haiku t5 6.0 against 6.06). A head that looks low is a 5 mm head in a shallower recess, standing proud, which is what R4's "only 5 mm has been printed" describes.
- **Bolt off the hole.** In a slot the largest clear circle can sit anywhere along it. The bolt now goes at the slot's middle when it fits there (Gemini 3.8 Flash t1: 0.53 mm off before).
- **Edge notches.** A caption line now says the bolt check took a notch open to the plate's edge for the bolt's hole.
- **Missing bearing.** The caption says when only one bearing has a seat. A falling piece now stops on the bed instead of dropping through it.
- **No-scene reasons** are in reader words, for example "the design is 2 printed parts, and the knob checks read only one". A wheel printed in two pieces says nothing saved shows how they fit together, so it isn't drawn assembled.
- **Viewer.** The layer checkboxes now follow what the fit hides and shows, and the fit no longer re-shows a layer the reader unticked. Reduced motion restores the hidden layers after a fit that seats. After a fit that seats, the button reads "Play the fit" again. Each kind of part's caption says how it's turned: a knob is shown with the face the nut goes in from on top, since it has no one way up.
- A scene with no fit says the hardware is drawn where it would sit, and that no recorded print says whether it goes in.

Checked as well, with nothing to fix:
- **Estoa scenes**, the 28 first prints: the plug head is inside the printed parts by 0.0 mm³ in all 21 scenes that draw it. The disk and the board overlap plastic only where the design itself puts them there, and a check fails on that: deepseek-v4-pro t3's disk (R1, "printed plastic stands where the disk's board is"), Gemini 4 Argon t3's perfboard (R5, "sits in printed plastic") and GPT-6.1 Sol t4's (R5).
- **Keyboard only**: Tab reaches the canvas, which shows a focus ring. Arrow keys turn the view without scrolling the page, and Tab leaves the canvas for the layer checkboxes and then Play the fit, which Enter starts.
- **Reduced motion** (`matchMedia` overridden in the page): a fit that seats shows its end state at once and the bolt comes back, ticked. While a fit plays, the bolt's box unticks, and a box the reader unticks during the play stays unticked afterwards.

Merged on top of #32 and #30. `SCENE_VERSION` went up to 5, so every key changed again. 1,023 designs kept main's renders and this PR's committed views, which were drawn from byte-identical inputs. The other 122 (every Estoa design and those whose saved files changed) had their views drawn again; their bytes match files already uploaded. The same 15 designs, redone from scratch, come out identical to the committed entries. `RESCUED` is the definition the checks page already has in `scripts/results_site.py`. The STL flag's test now allows a model's own part id, such as "fit_coupon", which `reader_words` leaves in quotes.

Codex's review found that a falling piece moved the wrong way. `place(entry, back)` puts a piece at `axis * -back`, and `-axis` is the way out, so a drop needs a positive distance; both the reduced-motion end state and the animation had it negative. Checked with reduced motion on GLM 5.3 Flash's M5 knob t5, with "Printed part" unticked: before the fix the nut hovered where the knob had been, and after it the nut lies on the bed grid. Haiku 5.5's wheel t3 (the one "falls" wheel) now ends with its bearing on the bed.

Codex's review of the assembled-views PR beneath this one (#26) found two more faults. One was already fixed here: the bolt's head no longer plays as seating when its own check (R4) isn't graded. The other is fixed here: when only the view's inputs change, the script redraws the view from each part's mesh in `folder/parts`, which isn't committed. If those files had gone it drew no view, reported the part as not built, and saved the design under a current key, so no later run would try again. It now builds the parts first, and a redraw that fails redoes the design in full instead of saving. A test covers both.
