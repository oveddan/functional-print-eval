## 2026-11-17: The eval is called Funk3D-Bench now (no result changed)

New name. It used to be "Functional 3D Printing Eval", or "Print-and-Build Eval" at the top of the reference. The first time it shows up it reads "Funk3D-Bench: can LLMs design functional 3D prints?", and just Funk3D-Bench after that.

Why this name: names starting with "Func" were already taken by function-calling benchmarks (FuncBenchGen, ComplexFuncBench), and FDM-Bench is a different 3D-printing benchmark. The README's prior work now mentions FDM-Bench.

Touched: the README title, the site's page titles, heading, tagline and footer, the checks page, and the reference title. Repo name and URLs are the same. Nothing under `results/`, the graders or the scorecard moved.
