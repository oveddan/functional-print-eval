What each model designed

Every design here came from one request and was graded by code against the test prints on record. None of these designs was printed. A "test coupon" is the small test piece a model designed and asked the person to print first. Click a trial to see each part in 3D, every check, and what went wrong.

"Not graded" means no recorded print can decide that check. It isn't a fail and it isn't a pass. "Declined" means the model said it couldn't design the part without a measurement nobody recorded; that's neither either. Checks on a test coupon are shown but don't count toward the verdict. The M5 spacer, a control every model passed, isn't shown. Built by scripts/results_page.py from the saved runs.

Where the failures are

Every check that didn't pass, by what would have let the model do better, then by what went wrong and whether it's a real failure. A trial with several problems counts once in each row. Click a row in the second table to show those trials.

What the coupon loop did

12 knob and wheel trials from three cheaper models were each given one more turn, twice over: once after hearing how their test coupon came out (from recorded prints only), once after only "take another look". The second sample added two fixes: sizes tighter than one that wouldn't go in are answered, and the turn gets more calls. Every design is graded with the coupon left out. Open the "Went through the coupon loop" group below to see each trial.

In the first sample, GLM 5.3 Flash's wheel t1 handed in no design after the report: every call stopped at the token limit. The second sample's extra calls fixed that.
